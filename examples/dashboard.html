SMALL RULES. MEANINGFUL BOUNDARIES.
More than “message received.”
01
The right sender.
Services authenticate and agree on a contract. A service with no permission cannot negotiate its way in.
02
The right data.
Schema and sensitive-field rules are checked before delivery. Violations can be quarantined with redacted evidence.
03
A clear outcome.
Accepted, duplicated or denied. Follow the broker’s decision and the receiver’s acknowledgement in the audit trail.


    
A SIMPLE IDEA, MADE EXPLICIT.
Your subject. 
Your rules.
A subject is a named destination for messages. Give it a policy; PigeonMQ checks every message against it.
  1. 1
    Define the policy
    Who can send and receive, which schema applies, and what data is allowed.
  2. 2
    Negotiate a contract
    The broker grants a service the operations its identity is allowed to perform.
  3. 3
    Send with confidence
    Publish under that contract. Inspect the actual outcome and its evidence.
 payments.authorize policy excerpt
// A few rules for a payment message.
{
  "name": "payments.authorize",
  "intents": ["authorize_payment"],
  "regionPolicy": {
    "allowedRegions": ["uk", "eu"]
  },
  "data": {
    "classification": "pci",
    "forbiddenFields": ["card.pan"]
  },
  "delivery": {
    "idempotency": { "required": true }
  }
}
